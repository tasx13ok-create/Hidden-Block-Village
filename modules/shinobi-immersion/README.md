# Shinobi Immersion 0.5.0

This is a standalone add-on. It depends on nothing; when the Naruto Core (`valeorders`) is present, it bridges to Core's `MoveCost` by reflection, so movement spends real NarutoMod chakra through the verified escrow. Without Core, movement costs a little hunger instead.

| Feature | Side | Config (`shinobi-immersion.cfg`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Water-walking.** Stand and run on water while not sneaking (sneak to dive). Costs chakra each second, and you sink when it runs out. Ripples and soft splashes. | both, paid on the server | `waterWalk`, `waterWalkCost` |
| **Wall run (up).** Sprint (or dodge) into a wall you face and hold jump: you run up it for up to 0.8 s (about 4-5 blocks), and if the top is in reach you pull yourself onto it automatically. A jump pressed up to 4 ticks before touching the wall still counts. Costs chakra per run. | client movement, paid on the server | `wallRun`, `wallRunCost` |
| **Wall run (along).** Jump toward a wall beside you at speed while holding forward: you run along it for up to 2 s, rising a little then sinking gently. The camera leans away from the wall, the view widens slightly, and other players see your body lean off it. Release forward or sneak to drop off. | client movement, paid on the server (entry + 1/s) | `wallSideRun`, `wallSideRunCost`, `cameraTilt` |
| **Wall jumps.** Press jump while on a wall, or within 4 ticks of leaving it, to kick off it: away, up and onward. Chain up to 3 between walls before landing; a wall can't be run twice in one airtime, so there is no endless climbing. | client movement, paid on the server | `wallJump`, `wallJumpCost`, `maxWallJumps` |
| **Wall slide.** Fall while pressing into a wall and you slide down it at a steady pace, with dust and a scrape; the server limits the fall damage. | client movement, server fall protection | `wallSlide` |
| **Dodge feel (on Core's dodge).** Core waits for the server before it moves you; on the same tick you press its dodge key you get a lean into side dodges, a view punch and a whoosh. The push itself, its chakra cost and cooldown stay Core's. Once it lands you leave an afterimage trail and kick up dust on landing. A dodge cancels a wall run and can start one. | client | `dodgeFeel`, `cameraTilt` |
| **Dodge hop.** Jump within 6 ticks of a ground dodge to keep most of its momentum through the jump. | client | `dodgeHop` |
| **Underwater sound.** World sounds are muffled (quieter and lower) while your head is underwater. | client | `underwaterSound` |
| **Cave echo.** A quiet delayed echo plays in enclosed spaces. Music, UI and weather are untouched, and echoes never echo. | client | `caveEcho` |
| **Ambience.** Sparse, quiet cues chosen by biome, time and height: forest birds, night rustle, cave drips and bats, water by shores, high wind. Placed around the listener and controlled by the Ambient volume slider. | client | `ambience`, `ambienceVolume` |
| **Combat sound layers.** NarutoMod, Core, armor, Kabuto and sword sounds get ±6% pitch variation, and loud ones get a soft low tail 2 ticks later. | client | `combatLayers` |
| **Perfect dodge.** If a hit is avoided within the first 6 ticks of Core's dodge (read from Core's own Dodge.Pre event on the server), the attacker is slowed for 1.5 s, you get a short speed boost, some chakra is refunded through Core's `MoveCost.restore`, and you see a brief edge flash and hear a chime. | server, with a client cue | `perfectDodge`, `perfectDodgeRefund` |
| **Elemental reactions.** Elements are read from the damage type and source. Fire on a wet target makes steam: the flames go out and the hit does 70% damage. Lightning on a wet target chains to up to 3 nearby wet mobs for 30%. Wind on a burning target spreads fire to nearby mobs (not players). Earth on a burning target snuffs the fire. Blocks are never changed. | server | `elementalReactions` |
| **Boss cues.** A boss is any entity that isn't vanilla `isNonBoss`. A red ring appears at its feet as it starts an attack. At 65% and 30% health the arena reacts with a roar, dust rings and a shockwave that pushes players back without hurting them. | server | `bossCues` |
| **Hit-stop.** Heavy blows (6+ damage after armor) and finishing blows hold the camera for 2-3 frames, then kick it slightly. Only the rendered view moves, never your aim. | server cue, client camera | `hitStop`, `hitStopDamage` |
| **Guard break.** Five blocked hits within 3 s break a raised shield: it goes on a 3 s cooldown and you're slowed briefly. Only hits the shield faces count. | server | `guardBreak` |
| **Stagger.** Four player hits on a mob within 2 s stagger it for about a second (bosses need eight and recover faster), with a 5 s cooldown. | server | `stagger` |
| **Low health.** Below 25% health you hear a heartbeat that quickens as health drops, the screen edges darken and pulse with it, and world sound is muffled slightly. | client | `lowHealth` |
| **Chakra exhaustion.** Below 5% chakra (Core's real reading) you move a little slower and breathe hard until you're back to 15%. Off without Core. | server, with a client cue | `chakraExhaustion` |
| **Ledge grab.** Jump at a ledge 1.2-2.6 blocks above your feet and hold jump to pull up onto it. It needs two free blocks above the ledge and costs a little chakra. | both, paid on the server | `ledgeGrab`, `ledgeGrabCost` |
| **Landing roll.** Sneak as you land a 3.5-24 block fall: you roll forward with a quick camera dip and take 40-70% of the fall damage. | both | `landingRoll` |
| **Canopy and roof running.** Sprinting on leaves, stairs or slabs is 15% faster. This is a temporary modifier that is never saved. | both | `canopyRun` |
| **Weather.** Rain makes fire hits steam (targets are wet) and widens lightning chains to 6 blocks and 5 targets. Storms, high ground and dry-biome gusts carry dust on a wind that turns slowly through the day. Compass player range drops to 64 in rain and 32 in storms. | both | `weather` |
| **Footsteps.** Softer steps on grass, sand, snow and wool, a gravel crunch, and splashes when wading in shallow water. Sneaking is already silent in vanilla. | client | `footsteps` |
| **Village life.** By day, with two or more villagers or mod NPCs nearby, you hear distant chatter, smithing and doors. Nothing plays at night. | client | `villageLife` |
| **Squads.** `/squad invite <player>`, `accept`, `leave` and `list` (up to 6 players; vanilla scoreboard teammates count too). Squadmates show on the compass at any distance in the same dimension. A left-edge panel shows each one's health pips and distance, and a downed teammate (20% or less) pulses red. Squads last for the session. | server sync, client HUD | `squads` |
| **Sparring.** `/spar <player>`, then the other player types `/spar accept`. After a 3-2-1 countdown you fight, but a blow that would take either of you below 1 health ends the match instead, so nobody dies or loses items. It ends on `/spar forfeit`, moving 48+ blocks apart, a dimension change, logout or 5 minutes. Needs PvP on. | server | `sparring` |
| **Compass bar.** Cardinal directions, 15° ticks, a spawn or bed marker and nearby players (128 blocks) with distance. Hidden in menus, with F1 and on the debug screen. | client | `compassBar` |

**Checks:** 60 pure checks (`gradlew check`). The client run loaded it with Core 0.3.16 and the armor mod with no exceptions, wrote the config and showed the compass rendering.

**Not yet verified in game:** surface water-walking (the probe pool was deep) and wall-running. Both need a hands-on test.

0.5.0 passes 131 headless rule checks and a scripted course in the real client with Core 0.3.16 loaded (14/14): run up and over a 6-block wall onto its top, the server seeing the run, a 14-tick side run covering about 6 blocks, a wall jump off it, a 124-tick slide down 16 blocks landing with 0.2 damage, instant dodge feel on Core's key, Core's dodge recognised, a perfect dodge through Core's grace window, and a dodge hop keeping 0.90 blocks/tick against 0.06 without it. The test machine has no audio and no second player, so sounds and other players' view of you still need a hands-on check.
