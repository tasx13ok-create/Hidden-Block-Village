# Play the introductory academy in Core 0.3.10

This candidate adds six introductory exercises and two field contracts to the existing game. It uses an original, deliberately simple Minecraft campus with five named vanilla-villager guides. It does **not** complete the ten-stage campaign in `design/ACADEMY-CAMPAIGN.md`, replace the five starter schools with their final distinct mechanics, or integrate the friend's private CustomNPCs exports.

## Start a separate local sandbox

Build the complete current source using `docs/build-modules.md`. On Windows, run `tools/start_academy_preview.py` with `--java8-home`, `--installer`, `--minecraft-install` and `--support-mods` pointing to existing local inputs. The support folder must contain the inspected Essential, OptiFine G5 and JEI 4.22.0.1034 JARs. These third-party binaries are not published by this repository. The script verifies build outputs and the pinned official Forge installer, creates a **new** profile/world under `local/academy-preview-0310`, binds the server to loopback port 25577, builds the campus and launches its client. An existing destination is refused. Use `--destination` and `--port` for another isolated sandbox.

The original inspection client/world stays separate. This offline-name sandbox is for local play only; it is not a public authentication/server deployment configuration. The source build and native verification instructions remain the authoritative reproduction route; check the current handoff for the exact tested source and artifact hashes.

For another disposable world, an operator runs `/valeacademy build <x> <floor-y> <z>` in the overworld. The command loads only the bounded campus footprint, then refuses existing blocks, tile entities, actors, an existing course, unsupported schemas or world-border crossings. It never clears terrain. Pick an empty 65×65×10 volume immediately above solid flat ground. The default sandbox uses floor `(0,4,0)`. Place only one course per world. No operator permission is needed for the player route.

## Player route

Use a living **survival** player. Right-click a named guide, open chat and click the aqua response. `/valeacademy` shows the current objective; the issued journal also leads to academy/contract information. Coordinates below assume the default sandbox.

1. **Arrival:** meet `Dispatch Keeper - Welcome` at `(-24,5,0)` and enroll. Receive a journal and two rations. A full inventory queues these items for delivery.
2. **Orientation:** meet `Aki - Academy Mentor` at `(-16,5,0)`. Choose fire, water, earth, wind or lightning. These map to the existing Ember, Current, Flint, Gale and Storm ward engines. An existing chosen ward is preserved. Equip all four issued matching armor pieces. Keep the issued saber and technique codex.
3. **Movement:** walk over the blue lapis markers in order: `(-20,5,12)`, `(-12,5,24)`, `(0,5,24)`. Return to Aki and review the exercise. Ordered checkpoint progress survives reconnect; flying, riding and large sampled movement jumps do not count. This is a walking exercise, not the planned advanced tree/water course.
4. **Defense:** ask Aki to prepare your personal construct. Enter the sandstone ring around `(0,5,8)`. It marks your position, warns for two seconds and strikes. Move at least two blocks from the mark to demonstrate spacing. For guard, hold the issued saber, sneak and right-click during the warning, then receive its strike. For substitution, use the issued Reed Escape scroll to learn it; hold its technique, sneak-use twice with the practice cooldown between uses, then use without sneaking to arm it before receiving a strike. Keep enough resolve; let it recover between attempts. The construct cannot kill its owner and grants no dummy-kill XP. All three defenses are required, in any order. Return to Aki and review.
5. **First art:** keep the matching armor equipped, hold the technique codex and select your ward's introductory lance through `/vale arts`. Aim at your own construct inside the ring and cast three times, allowing resolve and cooldown recovery. Only accepted contact with that construct counts. Return to Aki and review. The current existing lances share an aimed-hit engine with ward effects; the final five distinct nature lessons remain open work.
6. **Provisions:** meet `Jun - Provisions` at `(16,5,0)`, collect the one-time bread/beef/carrot kit, then use the kitchen's real crafting table near `(14,5,8)`. Craft bread + cooked beef + carrot into two travel rations. Return to Jun and deliver one. An uncrafted ration alone cannot satisfy the exercise. If you consume/lose the ingredients, ordinary Minecraft ingredient gathering remains available; the unique kit is not reissued.
7. **The Missing Dispatch: First Delivery:** meet `Ren - Courier` at `(24,5,32)` and accept the dispatch. Visit the `Mission Officer` at `(24,5,0)` to collect this run's sealed dispatch in the server journal, then return to Ren, deliver and claim. There is no transferable parcel item to duplicate.
8. **Roadside Ambush:** after claiming the dispatch, accept the ambush from Ren. Defeat the two owner-bound field raiders in the far yard, then claim at Ren or the Mission Officer. Reopening the dialogue resumes the same run. Reconnect removes active actors while retaining earned mission progress; speak to Ren again to spawn only the remaining number. A new-run cooldown prevents rapid abandon/spawn farming.

Academy checkpoint rewards total 300 XP and five merit. The first delivery grants 125 XP and one merit; ambush grants 175 XP and two merit plus ordinary accepted enemy-kill XP. Repeated claims reuse stable receipts or are refused. These are introductory exercises and contract prototypes, not the final escort branches, civilian rescue, unique charge/channel encounter or graduation story.

## Validation boundaries

`verifyAcademy` covers progress ordering, replay, caps, persistence copies and future-schema preservation. `verifyJeiCatalog` covers empty/nonempty removal batches and error propagation. The private Forge fixture exercises actual resident interaction events, survival armor/items, damage/cancellation, Reed Escape, arts, recipe pickup, full-inventory issuance, mission kills and player logout/reload. It never ships in production JARs. Core 0.3.10 native receipts require its academy completion label, exact mod roster and hashes.

Native fixture success, a network-connected graphical client and a visible human walkthrough are distinct checks. See `docs/current-handoff.md` and the version-specific evidence record for results actually observed. Copied production-world migration/restart, two-client fairness, Magma/plugins and Bisect acceptance require their own tests.
