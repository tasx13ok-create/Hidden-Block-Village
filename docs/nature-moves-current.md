# Starter nature techniques

Implemented in `Arts` and `village.vale.nature` during the November 13 sprint. These are original gameplay adaptations using Naruto's release terminology; they are not claims of a complete canon move roster. Stable saved technique IDs remain unchanged. Only Ember, Current, Flint, Gale and Storm shape-zero arts change here. Existing higher shapes and the other four schools retain their previous implementations.

Choose a nature through the academy, reach level two, equip four matching issued armor pieces, and hold the technique codex. Sneak/use cycles the codex; `/vale art select <stable ID>` and the existing ability bar select the same authoritative art. The bar may cast while the codex is carried. Other schools still use the existing study unlock. Costs below are chakra, stored in the existing resolve account; the existing form discount applies. An accepted cast also has one second of shared art recovery.

## Accepted effects and protection

Living targets pass the existing team, hostile/NPC and PvP authority through `ArsenalAccess`. Native damage must actually reduce health/absorption and survive the existing final cancellation checks before elemental aftermath or academy contact credit is applied. Target lines check loaded geometry and world borders before ray tracing. Earth additionally uses native `onPlayerMultiBlockPlace`, spawn/edit permission and entity occupancy checks.

Chakra and art cooldowns are reserved and saved before effect callbacks. A per-player cast lock blocks recursive entry. A failed/canceled cast refunds its reservation and restores only unchanged reserved cooldown values. Observed irreversible health loss keeps its charge even if a later callback throws. Multi-target work rechecks the player/world/equipment/school between contacts. A callback cannot turn subsequent contacts into authorized hits after removing the caster's requirements. Interrupted Earth rollback continues the other cells, saves any remaining recovery lease, and keeps the charge/deadline when expected cover remains or a touched cell becomes unloaded; it then rethrows the native failure.

Earth has a dedicated registered block, `valeorders:nature_cover`, using Minecraft's own cobblestone model. There is no inventory item, mining drop, silk-touch drop or pick-block result. Pistons cannot move it. The cover is short-lived physical collision/occlusion, not a chat-only buff. Blasts retain still-leased cover blocks for their short lifetime; terrain outside the cover follows native explosion behavior.

Per-dimension `vale_nature_cover` saved data records owner, expiry, original air and expected cover registry names. The journal is saved before placement callbacks. Cleanup never forces a chunk load. When a chunk is loaded, expiry restores only a cell still matching the expected cover with no tile entity; an intervening player/mod replacement or tile entity is retained. An unloaded lease remains journaled until loaded cleanup is possible. A reversed world clock also expires leases conservatively. Future/invalid schemas are kept opaque and read-only rather than rewritten. Existing terrain, inventories and tile data are never copied into or overwritten by a new wall.

Only successful nonempty protected Earth placement calls `AcademyCampaign.defensiveArt(player,key,center)`. The academy owns course/stage/nature/proximity/construct validation and objective credit. Damage arts call the existing accepted-contact callback. Defensive Earth is not forced to damage a dummy to complete practice.

## Verification and remaining scope

`NatureRulesTest` is independent of Minecraft bootstrap and passed **92 assertions** under the prepared JDK 8. It checks all four wall orientations, bounded six-cell geometry, wide Water versus narrow Fire targeting, range/backward/nonfinite/zero-look rejection, per-owner/world budgets and conservative unloaded/tile/player-edit restoration policy.

Private native helper: `village.vale.nature.NatureNativeChecks.run(EntityPlayerMP)` returns its assertion count and is designed for the existing isolated `vale-recovery-test` harness. It exercises actual issued armor/codex casts, burst/wave boundaries, native attack/hurt/damage cancellation, pre-reservation and recursive callbacks, post-damage exceptions, occlusion, wet Lightning interaction, arrow ownership/cancellation, physical Earth collision, canceled placement rollback, an intentionally throwing native neighbor callback with retained-cover settlement and recovery, restart serialization, expiry, player glass/chest edits, unknown schemas, occupied tiles and unloaded geometry. Root owns compiling and running that helper with the complete concurrent sprint build; do not infer a native pass solely from this source document.

The combined Core 0.3.11 build and full 1,704-assertion disposable Forge run now pass, including this helper. Exact source/artifact/private-fixture hashes are recorded in `release/evidence/core0311-verification.json`. The cancellation fixture compares actual vanilla native knockback rather than incorrectly demanding that late-canceled contact has zero motion; unloaded-space setup avoids a collision-querying test teleport loading its own target chunk.

These effects currently use native particles, sound, item swing, damage and world blocks. They do not supply new hand-seal/body animation, a steerable water projectile, a traveling fireball, a complete elemental reaction matrix, all projectile classes, or complete fan/seal remakes. Visual readability and feel still require human client playtesting in the approved full assembly. Claims of release readiness must reference the built jar and the root's actual compile/native receipt.
