# Elemental floor marker candidates

`python -m tools.elemental_dungeons.markers --input-dir local/v17-intake --output-dir local/<new-directory>` creates five **new private schematic copies**, plans and verification logs. It requires the five exact recovered v17 hashes. It never changes the source schematics or a world. The output directory must not exist. These are small visual markers, not complete elemental dungeon conversions, NPC encounters or a new release.

The owner mapped Sakura to Earth, Cloud to Lightning, Sand to Wind, Mist to Water and Nether to Fire. The original generator places distinct colored hardened-clay floor motifs at previously measured platform locations and separate sparse accents in the encounter areas. Each selected block must match an allowed original full block, have two clear air cells above and contain no tile at the floor or headroom. Floor-height full-block replacements avoid introducing a collision obstruction. The Nether marker is a smaller 3×3 motif at a separate clear patch, preserving the nearby glowstone and carpet. Encounter patterns retain more than half of each sampled floor patch. `tools.world_merge.edits` enforces exact source hashes, exact expected cells, no overwrite, and post-write comparison of all block/data cells, tile entities and other tags. This does not apply the proposed C1 repairs.

These static checks do not establish native movement, lighting, visual quality, player understanding, encounter behavior, source-to-world integration or save/restart behavior. Inspect the copies in a disposable matching 1.12.2 world before using them. Preserve the source, frozen world and original private materials.
